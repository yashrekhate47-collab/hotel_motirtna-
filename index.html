Our Story

Where Taste Meets Tradition

Hotel Motiratna is more than a restaurant — it is a promise of honest, flavourful food served with genuine Marathi hospitality. Our tagline says it all: “याच लागते हॉटेल मोतीरत्न बघायला नाही हो जेवायला” — come here not to merely look, but to eat.

From our signature Veg & Non-Veg Thali to slow-cooked mutton rassa and home-style chicken curry, every plate is prepared with care, using fresh ingredients and recipes that have been perfected over the years.

Whether you are a vegetarian seeking a complete wholesome meal or a non-veg lover craving bold spices, Motiratna welcomes you with open arms and full plates.
